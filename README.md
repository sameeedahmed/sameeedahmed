# Hi, I'm Sameed Ahmed 👋

Research fellow at **DISI, University of Bologna (Cesena)**, working on **indoor localization and
smartphone-guided building evacuation** in the *Reduce Meteo Hazard* project.

### What I work on
- **Indoor localization** — UWB, Bluetooth LE, Wi-Fi and GPS measured head-to-head in a real
  building; anchor placement (coverage, CRLB, simulated annealing); position fusion (Gauss–Newton,
  EKF, particle filter).
- **Evacuation guidance** — an Android app that plans the way out on the phone, guides the person
  turn by turn with voice and haptics, and sends an SOS with their floor and room — designed from
  a study of emergency-app and evacuation-interface research.
- **Simulation** — an evacuation and localization simulator to compare technologies, placement
  algorithms and protocols on real and synthetic buildings.
